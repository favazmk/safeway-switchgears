How a Safeway panel is built

Engineered layer by layer, built to last.

  1. 01

    Enclosure

    Electrogalvanized steel, GRP, aluminium or stainless steel. Floor or wall mounted, IP31 to IP65.

  2. 02

    Internal separation

    Form 2b, Form 3b and Form 4b Type 6 compartments that protect people and keep systems running.

  3. 03

    Copper busbar system

    Busbars rated up to 2500A with 36kA, 50kA and 65kA short-circuit ratings.

  4. 04

    Protection devices

    Breakers and switching devices from ABB, Schneider Electric, Siemens, Eaton and more.

  5. 05

    Wiring & control

    PLC, VFD and conventional control circuits for pumps, fans, AHU and FAHU applications.

  6. 06

    Tested & energised

    Built to IEC 61439-1 & 2 and ready for TAQA, DEWA and FEWA approvals.
